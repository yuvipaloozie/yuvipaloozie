# Hi, I'm Yuvraj!
I studied chemical engineering and now work on applied ML problems in industrial systems and biomedical research. I am particularly interested in graph neural networks, time-series modelling and computer vision, especially when the model can be tested against a physical process or an experiment.

### Education
* **M.Sc. Computational Data Analytics** (expected 2027) | Georgia Institute of Technology
* **B.S. Chemical Engineering** (2024) | Georgia Institute of Technology

### Ongoing Projects and Research
* **Automated Osteoclast Segmentation:** _(In cooperation with Sarah Szabo and PCOM GA)_ Developing a TransU-Net architecture with diffusion models and semi-supervised learning to segment and count osteoclasts from microscope imagery to assist in medical research.
* **Drug Repurposing for Osteoclastogenesis Inhibition:** _(In cooperation with Sarah Szabo and PCOM GA)_ Building a mechanism knowledge graph for osteoclast drug-repurposing research. The molecular-modeling direction includes Graph Attention Networks on ChEMBL data and an adversarial LLM to help shortlist FDA-approved candidates; validated repurposing predictions remain future work.
* **Reinforcement Learning for Process Control:** Creating and testing RL agents for chemical-process control across processes of varying complexity.
* **Knowledge Hypergraphs for Manufacturing Plants:** Developing a pipeline for zero-shot P&ID digitization and manufacturing-process hypergraphs for agent-based applications.
